(1) Arsitektur/metode inti:
LLM penasihat saja. Usulkan parameter, tanpa eksekusi langsung, tanpa akses store. Orchestrator putuskan deterministik. (hlm.5-6)
Tiga hard gate wajib: L2 autentikasi+resolusi tenant, L3 otorisasi tool berbasis peran, L5 gate validasi `V(p, s, tr,ρ r)`. Satu gagal = tolak. (hlm.1-2, hlm.7-8)
Empat filter advisory: L1 sanitasi input, L4 scoping retrieval, L6 monitor eksekusi, L7 kepatuhan output. Sumbang skor `τk(r)`, agregat `τ(r) = minkτk(r)`. Triase saja, tanpa override gate. Ambang `θ=0.8`, `θreview=0.3`. (hlm.6-7)
Isolasi tenant logis `data(ri)∩data(rj)=∅` untuk `tri≠trj` di tiap tahap. Klaim berlaku di control-plane logis di bawah A1 dan A5. (hlm.7-8)
Audit append-only tiap layer: policy, retrieval, validasi, eksekusi, `τ`. (hlm.7, hlm.12)

(3) Keterbatasan verbatim:
Kutipan 1 (hlm.1): "The hard gates provide three structural guarantees (tenant isolation, tool-allowlist enforcement, schema conformance) specified as deterministic predicates and verified at the logical control-plane level; they do not defend against within-scope intent manipulation, in which an authorized role invokes an authorized tool with schema-valid parameters for a malicious purpose."
Kutipan 2 (hlm.11): "For within-scope attacks by capable models (authorized roles with well-formed but malicious parameters), no hard gate engages; the defense reduces to L1 (heuristic, 10.2% flag rate, 0% solo-rejection rate) and L7 (output filtering), which is insufficient."

(4) Evaluasi K1-K5:
K1 trust dinamis: tidak penuhi. `τ` statis per-request, min agregasi. Session simpan akumulasi skor tanpa aturan decay/belajar. (hlm.6-7, hlm.9)
K2 konflik tipologi: sebagian. Punya T1-T5, mapping OWASP, A1-A5, gap intent-to-action. Tanpa tipologi konflik tujuan antar agen. (hlm.4-5, hlm.11-12)
K3 biaya-keputusan: tidak penuhi. Tanpa model biaya/latensi. Satu klaim zero latency cost, eskalasi manusia via `θ`. (hlm.1, hlm.7)
K4 policy terpadu: penuhi. `policyreq` di L3 beda dari `policytool` di L5. Komposisi tunggal wajib, tanpa bypass. (hlm.7-8, hlm.4)
K5 ketahanan: struktur kuat, intent rapuh. Tahan cross-scope, jebol within-scope 30%-70%. (hlm.1-2, hlm.11)
